Find out which settings really change your FPS in Deadlock

Build gameinfo.gi and video.txt configs, benchmark them on the same replay moment, and see how each one compares with your current setup. Free, open source, Linux.

pipx install git+https://github.com/itchyfeetleech/Deadlock-Performance-Lab.git && dpl

Needs Python 3.11+ (most distributions have it). dpl opens the app in your browser. Try the example report first: no game needed.

Measure it, don't guess

Config guides tell you what to change. This tells you what each change does on your machine.

Build configs your way

Start from your own files, a Sqooky OptimizationLock preset (fetched from GitHub when you pick it) or an imported file. Tick settings, grouped and described, with switches and sliders.

Test many settings separately

Every ticked setting, and every value you list for it, becomes its own config, so you see what each one does alone. Then re-test the best few with more rounds.

Numbers you can trust

Your current setup is measured before and after every round, so noise and drift show up. Results are ranked with the noise floor marked, and verdicts need repeated rounds.

Four steps

The app checks your PC and explains each step as you go.

  1. Set upPaste one line into Deadlock's Steam launch options and pick a replay moment.
  2. ConfigsTick what you want to test and preview the exact file changes.
  3. BenchmarkChoose Quick look, Shortlist or Confirm. It tells you the launches and time up front.
  4. ResultsRead the ranking, open the full report, re-test the winners and share a ZIP.

Safe by design

  • Your files come back. They're backed up first and restored after every capture, even after a crash.
  • Local replays only. Launched with -insecure, never online matches.
  • Stays on your machine. The app listens only on localhost and downloads only the presets you pick.
  • Honest verdicts. Improved or regressed needs five rounds, stable baselines and your confirmation.
