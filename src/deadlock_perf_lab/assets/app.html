    Your configs

    A config is a gameinfo.gi and/or video.txt to test against your current setup. Start from your own files, one of Sqooky's OptimizationLock presets (downloaded from GitHub when you pick one) or a file you import, then tick the settings to change. Your game only uses a config while a benchmark runs.
